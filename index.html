Scientific Yield Benchmarks: 4 Tonnes Baseline vs 12–16 Tonnes Precision Target

Comparing conventional rain-fed 4-tonne (4,000 kg) baselines against precision drip-fertigated raised-bed production in Karnataka.

Conventional Baseline

4 Tonnes (4,000 kg) / Acre

40 Quintals/Acre (~10 Tonnes/Hectare)

  • Rain-fed & Flat Ridge Benchmark: The prevailing baseline yield across Karnataka for unfertilized, rain-dependent or traditional flood-furrow farming without bio-inoculants.
  • Subterranean Clump Mass: At 4,000 kg with ~28,000 plants/acre, each clump averages only 0.14 kg due to root-zone compaction and moisture fluctuations.
  • Nutrient & Soil Limitations: Traditional broadcast fertilizer suffers up to 50% leaching and volatilization, restricting primary and secondary finger branching.
  • Cured Dry Output: At 18% dry recovery, 4 tonnes fresh produces approximately 720 kg (7.2 quintals) of cured fingers, returning minimal net profit.

Broad Raised Bed (BBF) Specifications

120 cm (4.0 ft) Top Width
● ● ●
Height: 25–30 cm
30 cm Furrow / Pathway (Rapid Monsoon Runoff)
  • Bed Spacing: 150 cm (5.0 ft) center-to-center.
  • Plant Grid on Bed: 3 rows per bed, spaced 35 cm row-to-row × 20 cm plant-to-plant.
  • Total Beds / Acre: ~22 to 24 beds of 100 meters length.
  • Plant Density: 28,000 – 30,000 plants per acre.
  • Monsoon Safety: Raised profile keeps developing rhizomes 25 cm above standing furrow water.
